2 Simplified banking 
With simplified banking, your consumer Eligible Deposit and Investment (EDI) accounts will be linked to create one 
Combined Average Monthly Balance (CAMB) to determine your features and benefits. Higher CAMBs for deposit 
account owners may allow you to join Relationship Tiers and receive Monthly Service Fee and Non-Citi ATM fee 
waivers. Please refer to section 3 Relationship Tiers and section 5 Products. Simplified banking customers who own 
multiple deposit accounts also may receive one Account Statement as explained in section 4 Account Statements. 
Your CAMB is the total of the End of Day Available Now balances for all your EDI account(s) in a calendar month 
divided by the number of days in that month. Your CAMB is based on the calendar month which may be different 
than your Statement Period. You must be the owner (or beneficial owner) of an EDI account for the account to 
contribute toward your CAMB. Not all accounts appearing on your Account Statements are EDI accounts. You 
may contact us to learn which of your EDI accounts contribute to your CAMB. If you only have one EDI account, 
your CAMB is based on the Average Monthly Balance of that EDI account. Entities share CAMB with other 
accounts under the same title. EDI accounts with the same Tax Identification Number as consumers, including 
those owned by revocable and irrevocable trusts, may be included in CAMB.
Account linking in simplified banking replaces any prior written or oral linking requests or agreements made before 
your accounts were converted to simplified banking. Any existing package waivers, including fee waivers, were 
terminated when you or your accounts converted to simplified banking. Account linking in simplified banking has 
no effect on accounts you have linked for other purposes such as setting up and performing certain transactions.

fall below the Balance Range for three consecutive calendar months or you will be Re-Tiered 
Out. You may qualify for a Relationship Tier faster or maintain your Relationship Tier through

you are in a Relationship Tier or own a Citi Miles Ahead Savings account, you can request to change your 
Statement Period Date(s). Any change to your Statement Period Date will impact all Checking Plus account(s) on 
that Account Statement. Speak to a banker to learn more.

Appendix 5: General Funds Transfers Safety Tips
Help Protect Yourself from Wire and Other Funds Transfer Scams
Wire transfers are a fast way to send money to individuals and businesses. However, because they are fast 
and final they can also be a target for fraud. According to the Federal Bureau of Investigation, the incidents 
of wire transfer scams have increased significantly. Common scams include: phishing (attempts to obtain 
sensitive information such as account details by posing as a reputable company via email, text, or phone); 
family emergency (receipt of a message from a friend or family member claiming to be in need of money for an 
emergency); and IRS Impostors (fake IRS tax notices or individuals impersonating as IRS agents claiming you 
owe taxes). For additional recent and common scams, please visit the Federal Trade Commission web site at 
https:/ /www.consumer.ftc.gov/features/scam-alerts.
Scammers will often contact you unexpectedly and convince you to move your funds quickly. Because 
sending a wire or another type of funds transfer is like sending cash, it is unlikely you will get your money 
back if you’re scammed. You should also be aware that a wire transfer will be credited by the beneficiary 
institution on the basis of the account number provided and not on the basis of the name provided in your 
payment instruction. For these reasons it is important that you verify any request for you to transfer funds. 
Ask yourself these questions before proceeding and speak with a Citibank representative if you believe you 
may be the victim of a scam.
• Are you sending a transfer because someone claiming to be from Citibank or another financial institution
asked you to? Citi will never ask you to send funds to yourself or anyone else to stop fraud, to keep your funds
safe or to correct an issue.
• Are you sending a transfer because someone claiming to be from the Internal Revenue Service, law
enforcement, Social Security, Immigration, or a tech support company asked you to?
• Are you sending a transfer after someone contacted you unexpectedly for money (unsolicited phone call, text,
email, etc.) and pressured you to send funds right away?
• Are you sending a transfer to someone who provided funds transfer instructions through a messaging service,
social media or email?
• Are you sending a transfer after someone instructed you not to tell anyone about the funds transfer or
instructed you to lie about the reason for the funds transfer?
• Are you sending a transfer in response to someone claiming to be an investor and promising a high rate of
return and minimum risk on a digital currency investment (Cryptocurrency)?
• Are you sending the transfer after someone contacted you and claimed a loved one is in trouble and is in urgent
need of funds?
If any of these situations are the reason you are sending a transfer, your transfer may be part of a fraud scheme. 
We urge you to exercise caution and either reconsider sending the transfer or further verifying the transaction 
before proceeding.
By proceeding and submitting your funds transfer request, you acknowledge you are solely responsible for 
confirming all aspects of your funds transfer.

5.4.3 Citi Pr emium Boost for Citi Savings accounts
As of October 26, 2026, “Eligible Customers” who complete “Required Activities” for their Relationship Tier 
during a calendar month will receive the “Premium Boost” Interest Rate for their Relationship Tier starting on the 
first Business Day of the next calendar month, so long as they remain in a Relationship Tier in that next month. 
Customers must remain in a Relationship Tier the next month to receive the Premium Boost Rate.
Citi Premium Boost is available only for Citi Savings accounts. Citi Premium Boost is not available for Citi 
Accelerate Savings accounts, Certificates of Deposit, or any other interest-bearing accounts. Premium Boost 
Rates replace “Standard Rates” when applicable, are standalone rates, and do not combine with, add to, or 
increase any other Annual Percentage Yields (APYs) or interest rates.
Premium Boost Required Activities for Relationship Tiers are different from the requirements to attain and 
remain in Relationship Tiers.
5.4.3.1 Citi Pr emium Boost for Citi Savings accounts
Citi Savings accounts owned by Eligible Customers who complete Premium Boost Required Activities for their 
Relationship Tier during a calendar month will receive the Premium Boost Interest Rate applicable to their 
Relationship Tier starting on the first Business Day of the next calendar month for that month only. The Premium 
Boost Interest Rate applied to a Citi Savings account will be based on the customer’s Relationship Tier during 
the month the Premium Boost Interest Rate applies and not the Relationship Tier for the month customer met 
Premium Boost Required Activities. Eligible Customers must meet Premium Boost Required Activities for their 
Relationship Tier each month to receive a Premium Boost Rate for the following month. For example: 
• If a customer is in the Citigold Relationship Tier in January, they must meet Citigold Premium Boost Required 
Activities in January to receive a Premium Boost Interest Rate in February. If the customer is Re-Tiered to the 
Citi Priority Relationship Tier in February, they will receive the Citi Priority Premium Boost Interest Rate for the 
month of February. 
• If the customer meets the Citi Priority Premium Boost Required Activities in February, they will receive the Citi 
Priority Premium Boost Interest Rate in March. If the customer does not meet the Citi Priority Premium Boost 
Required Activities in March, they will receive the Standard Interest Rate for April.
• If the customer meets the Citi Priority Premium Boost Required Activities in February and is Re-Tiered Out of 
Relationship Tiers in March, they will not receive the Premium Boost Interest Rate in the month of March. 
Eligibility. “Eligible Customers” means customers who have met the following eligibility requirements on the last 
Business Day of the month at 10:30 pm ET: 
1. Be in the Citi Priority, Citigold, or Citigold Private Client Relationship Tier 
2. Own both an open Regular Checking account and an open Citi Savings account, and
3. Be opted in to Citi Premium Boost for Citi Savings accounts.

Savings Account APYs and Interest Rates 
“Annual Percentage Yield” (APY) as used in this document is a percentage rate reflecting the total amount of interest 
paid on an account, based on the current interest rate compounded daily for a period of one year assuming no fees, 
additional deposits or withdrawals, and the interest rate remains the same for the entire year. “Interest Rate” is the 
annual interest paid represented as a percentage assuming no change in rate and does not reflect compounding. 
“Annual Percentage Yield Earned” (APYE) shown for an account on the Account Statement is an annualized rate 
that reflects the total amount of interest paid on the account during the Statement Period. The APYE is calculated 
using the relationship between the interest posted to the account and the sum of the daily balances for those days 
where balance is above zero in the Statement Period divided by the number of days in the Statement Period. 
Annual Percentage Yields and interest rates for Savings accounts are variable, determined by Citibank at its sole 
discretion, and can change at any time without notice. This may affect the actual amount of interest earned.
