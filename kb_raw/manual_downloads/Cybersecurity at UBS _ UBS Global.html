Cybersecurity at UBS

How we keep y(our) data safe

Our number one priority is our clients and the safety of their assets and data at UBS.  

As the volume and sophistication of cyber-attacks in general grow, it’s more important than ever to stay one step ahead of online criminals. UBS continuously focuses resources and investments on critical cyber and information security capabilities. We have a vast array of both internal and external capabilities, aligned to global industry standards, in place to protect client’s assets and data.

Together let’s keep cyber criminals locked out.
