Fraud prevention and security at UBS

Unfortunately, scammers are also active in Switzerland. Be vigilant – the following tips will help protect you and your money.

“It sounded really urgent, so I didn’t question it...”

If a request arrives unexpectedly or seems suspicious, end the communication.

Learn more now and prevent fraud

How to protect yourself against fraud

Use the Access App

And only scan QR codes on the E-Banking login page, never from emails or text messages.

Know who’s calling

If you’re uncertain about who’s calling you, hang up and call back using an official number.

Deny access

Never allow strangers to access your computer remotely.

Stay skeptical

If an investment offer seems too good to be true, it probably is!
 

Have you noticed anything suspicious?

Please contact us immediately so we can assist you.

Report here

For your security in Digital Banking

Set up notifications for accounts and cards and report suspicious transactions immediately.

Enter ubs.com directly into the browser’s address bar to log in to E-Banking and avoid search engines.

Every time you log in to E-Banking, verify that your browser’s address bar contains .ubs.com/.

Make sure your software is always up to date by regularly installing updates.

Be aware of typical types of fraud

Understand typical types of fraud
  • Unrealistically high returns
  • Family member in distress
  • Urgent payment order
  • Fake invoices
  • Fake links
  • An apparent technical issue
