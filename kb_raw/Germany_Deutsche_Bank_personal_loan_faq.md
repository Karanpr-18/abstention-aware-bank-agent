# Deutsche_Bank (Germany) — personal_loan_faq
Source: https://www.deutsche.bank.in/en/loans/personal-loan-faqs.html

You pay the loan in equated monthly installments. The loan will be paid through standing instructions to debit your Deutsche Bank account with the EMI amount.
Once you submit your application form and supporting documents, we shall give approval within 7 working days provided everything is in order. All loan approvals are at the sole discretion of the bank.
You can choose to repay your loan any time from the date of disbursal of the loan; for applicable pre-payment terms, please refer to the Personal Loan Schedule of Charges.
