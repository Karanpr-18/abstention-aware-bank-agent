During the promotion period, the top 39 spenders with a minimum spend of S$4,000.00 from 1 
December 2025 till 31 December 2025 will be rewarded with the below gift. 
Tier Details Gift

Qualifying ICBC Mastercard Credit Cards (“Qualifying Card(s)”): 
a) ICBC Global Travel Mastercard Credit Card; and 
b) ICBC Chinese Zodiac Credit Card

This Promotion is open to ICBC Mastercard Credit Card Cardmembers of ICBC Singapore who satisfy 
the eligibility criterias below (“Eligible Cardmembers”):  
a) you are an individual who holds ICBC Mastercard Credit Card (ICBC Global Travel Mastercard

b) your Card account is satisfactorily conducted and remains active; and 
c) you are among the top 39 Eligible Cardmembers with the highest amount charged to the Card of 
qualifying transactions (minimum spend  of  S$4,000.00) within the Promotion Period.
